About

The best investment insight comes from knowing the subject.

Most investment clubs draw mainly from business and economics students. But many of the most important investment questions depend on other fields: whether a drug will succeed in clinical trials, whether a new technology works, how climate shifts will affect energy and agriculture, or how a policy change will reshape an industry.

ICC is built around that idea. Our research teams are led by students who study those fields, working alongside finance-trained members to turn their expertise into investment recommendations. No finance background is required to join.

The portfolio

A $150,000 multi-asset paper portfolio.

ICC manages a paper-traded portfolio invested at real market prices. It spans stocks, bonds and options rather than long-only stock picking, and every position is pitched by a research team and approved by the Investment Committee.

How it works

From research to position.

  1. Training

    New members learn valuation, portfolio construction and risk management. No finance background needed.

  2. Research

    Members join the team that matches their major and build an investment thesis.

  3. Pitch & vote

    Teams present a written recommendation; the elected Investment Committee votes.

  4. Monitor

    Teams track their positions and report results against the benchmark each semester.

What members get

Real research experience, across disciplines.

Published research

Written investment pitches and research reports to discuss with employers.

A path into investing

Students outside the business school apply their own field to real investment decisions.

Industry access

Speakers, case studies and networking with investment professionals, including people who came to finance from science and technology.
